How We Roll Out Fleet Control Across Your Network

Traditional distributors hand over equipment against a volume quota, with less fresh coffee and an opaque contract, then disappear until something breaks. We're not free equipment tied to a quota — we're a managed service: the machines, the coffee, the support and the dashboard are all our responsibility. Here's exactly how Telemetry-Driven Coffee Fleet Control rolls out.

Step 1: Site-Specific Volume Planning

  • Description: Before installation, our team visits each site in your network to plan machine placement, coffee volume and delivery schedule around your actual foot traffic.

  • Outcome: Right-sized equipment for every location — Dr. Coffee or Necta super-automatics for self-service sites, professional espresso machines where a barista is on shift.

Step 3: Dashboard & Reporting Setup

  • Description: We connect every machine to your online analytics dashboard, live for the whole network: cups sold, revenue, stock levels and error alerts, site by site.

  • Outcome: Real visibility instead of guesswork — you and we both see a fault or a low-stock site before it costs you a sale.

Step 4: Staff Training

  • Description: We train your staff on daily operation and basic maintenance as part of onboarding — enough to run the machine with confidence, on no fixed clock.

  • Outcome: Fewer user-error callouts, and cups that taste like they're supposed to, while our weekly technician visits handle everything beyond daily care.

Step 5: 24/7 Support & Same-Day Replacement

  • Description: If the dashboard flags an error, or your team calls our 24/7 line, we dispatch immediately — with the right part, or a full replacement machine ready to swap in.

  • Outcome: Minimal downtime: a technician on-site or a replacement machine delivered within 24 hours, same-day where possible, anywhere in Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast.
