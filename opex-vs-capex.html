The Process

How we deploy this for your site.

1

Step 1: The Site & Infrastructure Audit

We don’t just drop a machine on your counter and run. We look at your location's water hardness, power setup and peak-hour traffic before recommending equipment.
OutcomeA machine sized and configured for your actual conditions, not a generic default.
2

Step 2: Matching You To The Right Machine

Based on the audit, we install the machine that fits your volume — a Dr. Coffee or Necta super-automatic for self-service, or a professional espresso machine from La Spaziale, Nuova Simonelli, Rancilio, Iberital, Astoria or Fiorenzato for barista-operated service. No oversized machine gathering dust, no undersized one bottlenecking your rush hour.
OutcomeMachine throughput that actually matches customer demand.
3

Step 3: Filtration & Power Protection

We install water filtration calibrated to your site and, where needed, voltage protection for the machine — because hard water and unstable power are what actually cause early breakdowns.
OutcomeFewer breakdowns from scaling or power surges over the life of the machine.
4

Step 4: Staff & Barista Training

Untrained staff hurt coffee quality and wear the machine down faster. We train your front-of-house staff on daily operation and cleaning as part of onboarding — included, with no fixed clock to watch.
OutcomeFewer user errors and more consistent coffee, without adding a line item to your budget.
5

Step 5: Ongoing Support & Maintenance

Once you're live, our 24/7 support line and weekly technician visits keep the machine running. If something breaks between visits, call the line — we get a technician out or a replacement machine to you within 24 hours. ***
OutcomeA breakdown becomes a short interruption instead of days of lost sales, because the machine — and the risk — is still ours.
