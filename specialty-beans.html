Answers

Bean supply questions.

What's included in Anima Volitiva's bean supply service?

Your plan covers the coffee machine rental (Dr. Coffee, Necta or a professional espresso brand, matched to your business), a continuous supply of premium roasted beans from Covim S.p.A. (Italy) and Ukrainian roasters, weekly resupply and quality visits, and a 24/7 support line — priced with an individual quote for your volume.

Do we need to pay for the coffee machines upfront?

No. The machine is included in your rental plan rather than bought outright, so there's no upfront equipment purchase. You can also try the setup for 14 days with no prepayment before committing.

How fast is your technical support if our machine breaks down?

Our support line is open 24/7. If a machine breaks down, we send a technician or a replacement machine within 24 hours, same-day wherever possible.

How do you make sure the coffee is brewed correctly?

We calibrate grind and dose for your specific machine and beans during setup, train your staff on how to use it, and fine-tune it again during our regular visits.

How do you ensure the coffee quality remains consistent over time?

We restock beans and consumables weekly, and a technician checks and recalibrates the machine on the same visit, so quality doesn't quietly drift between deliveries.

Where do your premium coffee beans come from?

We source fresh-roasted premium coffee from Covim S.p.A. in Italy and from Ukrainian roasting partners — blends like Bellissima, LOT 101 and Ethiopia Yirgacheffe — chosen to suit your machine and your customers.

Is your service available outside of Kyiv Oblast?

We serve HoReCa, offices and retail across Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast. Get in touch and we'll confirm coverage and timing for your location.
