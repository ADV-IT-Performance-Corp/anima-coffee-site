Answers

Support questions.

What exactly is included in the service?

Your agreement covers equipment rental (Dr. Coffee and Necta super-automatics, or professional espresso machines for barista-operated setups), premium roasted coffee and consumables, 24/7 technical support, weekly quality visits and staff training — all individually quoted for your volume.

How fast will a technician arrive if our machine breaks down?

We get a technician on-site or a replacement machine to you within 24 hours, often the same day, for clients across Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast — minimizing your downtime and any lost revenue.

Is technical support available on weekends or outside normal business hours?

Yes, our support line is available 24/7, so your HoReCa or retail operation is covered whenever it's open. Hospitality doesn't keep business hours, and neither does our team.

Do we need to pay for the coffee machines upfront?

No. You rent the equipment rather than buying it, and every new client can start with a free 14-day trial with no prepayment before committing to a plan.

What kind of coffee machines do you provide?

Dr. Coffee and Necta super-automatics for offices, retail and self-service, plus professional espresso machines from La Spaziale, Nuova Simonelli, Rancilio, Iberital, Astoria and Fiorenzato for barista-operated HoReCa venues.

Is staff training included?

Yes — every client's team gets hands-on training on their specific equipment as part of onboarding, at no extra charge.

How do you keep coffee quality consistent over time?

Beyond initial training, a technician visits weekly to resupply beans and consumables and check the equipment, so quality stays consistent visit after visit.
