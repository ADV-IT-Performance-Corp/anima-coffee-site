Answers

Contract questions.

What exactly is included in the monthly service contract?

The monthly contract is a full-service coffee package: equipment rental (Dr. Coffee or Necta super-automatics, or a professional espresso machine for HoReCa venues), fresh-roasted premium coffee from Covim S.p.A. and Ukrainian roasting partners, staff training, a 24/7 support line, and weekly resupply and quality visits — all on one monthly invoice, sized to your volume.

Do we have to pay an upfront cost or deposit for the coffee machines?

No. It's a rental model, so there's no equipment to buy outright. You can also start with a free 14-day trial with no prepayment, and HoReCa clients get their first rental month free.

How fast is your technical support response time?

Our support line is staffed 24/7. If a machine breaks down, a technician repairs it or we deliver a replacement machine within 24 hours, same-day where possible.

What happens if our machine breaks down outside of regular business hours or on weekends?

The same guarantee applies around the clock. Our 24/7 support line covers weekends and holidays, so a breakdown never has to wait until Monday.

Is staff training included, and how long does it take?

Yes, hands-on staff training is included as part of our five-stage onboarding, so your team knows how to operate, clean and manage the espresso or super-automatic equipment. There's no fixed session length — we train until your team is confident.

How often do you check and maintain the rented machines?

A technician visits weekly to restock beans and consumables, check water filtration and keep every machine calibrated — not only when something breaks.

What kind of coffee do you supply with the monthly service contract?

Premium roasted coffee from Covim S.p.A. (Italy) and Ukrainian roasting partners — named blends like Bellissima, LOT 101 and Ethiopia Yirgacheffe — plus tea and the full set of consumables.
