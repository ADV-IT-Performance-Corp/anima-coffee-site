What the analytics dashboard tracks

* Cups sold: drink counts straight from the machine, so you can see what each location actually poured — by day and by drink.

* Revenue: what those pours are worth, so end-of-day reconciliation against your POS report is a comparison of two numbers, not guesswork.

* Stock: bean and consumables levels, so weekly resupply arrives before you run out rather than after.

* Errors: machine error states, so our technicians often know about a problem before your staff calls the support line.

How this fits your POS workflow

The dashboard is web-based and standalone — it does not require any particular POS, and it does not replace one. In practice, venues use it in three ways:

* End-of-day reconciliation. Your cashiers ring drinks up in Poster, Ultra, iiko or whatever system you run, exactly as they do today. The dashboard gives you the machine's own count of what was actually poured. If the register says 180 cappuccinos and the machine says 205, you know precisely where to look — no more arguing about "comped" drinks or missed ring-ups.

* Shrinkage and waste visibility. Because bean and milk consumption is tracked against pours, unexplained gaps between consumption, pours and register revenue become visible instead of disappearing into a black hole.

* Multi-site oversight. If you run several locations, the same dashboard shows every machine side by side — volumes, stock and errors — without anyone driving between sites to collect numbers.
