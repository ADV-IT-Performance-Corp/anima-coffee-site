The Anima Onboarding Methodology

We don't just drop off a machine and wish you luck. Anima Volitiva treats every placement as a structured rollout, refined since 2015 to get new sites running with no surprises. Before installation, our local Kyiv Oblast team reviews your traffic patterns, your POS software (Poster, iiko, Ultra or similar), and which machine from our lineup — Dr. Coffee, Necta, or one of our professional espresso brands — actually fits your space and volume. We then handle delivery, installation and water filtration setup, typically within 24 hours of confirming your order, and connect the machine into your POS so every pour is tracked from day one. Staff training is included with every placement, covering the machine itself and how it reports into your POS and our analytics dashboard, so your team is comfortable from the start. In the first week we run a dedicated on-site quality check, and after that a technician visits weekly for resupply and a routine quality pass — not just when something breaks. If a machine does fail, our 24/7 line puts a technician or a replacement unit on-site within 24 hours.
