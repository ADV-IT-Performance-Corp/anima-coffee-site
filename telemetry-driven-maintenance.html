    ### Why is Kyiv's municipal water supply particularly dangerous for WMF machines?

    Kyiv's water supply averages 15 to 20 dH, which contains high concentrations of calcium and magnesium. When heated inside a super-automatic boiler, these minerals precipitate rapidly, forming a thick layer of scale. This scale insulates the heating elements, causing thermal failure and restricting water flow. Our IoT system monitors exact water throughput down to the milliliter to guarantee filters are replaced before scale can form.
