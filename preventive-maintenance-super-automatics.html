The Process

How we deploy this for your site.

1

Step 1: Installation & Water Filtration Setup

We check your water line before installing your machine and fit the right filtration for your location. This is included with the rental, not sold separately — and it's the single biggest factor in preventing limescale damage to the boiler. *Outcome: A machine set up right from day one, protected against the water it's actually running on.*
2

Step 2: Weekly Resupply & Quality Visit

A technician visits your site every week — restocking beans and consumables, checking calibration, and servicing the machine before small issues turn into breakdowns. You don't have to notice something's wrong for us to catch it. *Outcome: Consistent cup quality and far fewer surprise service calls.*
3

Step 3: Staff Training

Untrained staff bypassing cleaning cycles or changing grinder settings is one of the most common causes of avoidable machine damage. Training for your team is included with every rental, so operators know how to run the machine correctly from day one. *Outcome: Fewer operator errors and less wear from misuse.*
4

Step 4: 24/7 Support Line

If something doesn't look or taste right, call us — any day, any hour. Our support line runs around the clock, not just during business hours, so a problem doesn't have to wait until Monday to get looked at. *Outcome: Issues get flagged and addressed before they grow into bigger problems.*
5

Step 5: Same-Day Replacement Guarantee

If a machine does break down, you're not left without coffee while you wait for a part. We provide a replacement machine within 24 hours — often the same day — while the original unit is repaired off-site. *Outcome: Minimal disruption to your business, and a working machine back in place fast.* ***
