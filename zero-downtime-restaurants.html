How We Keep Your Coffee Service Running

We don't just drop off a machine and walk away. Anima Volitiva runs a five-step onboarding for every restaurant partner: we plan the setup around your volume and floor layout; we deliver and install the machines and water filtration within 24 hours; we train your front-of-house team so any staff member can run the equipment correctly from day one; we run a quality check during your first week live to fine-tune grind, dose and workflow; and we review the full first month with you to confirm the setup is earning its keep. After that, it's a weekly rhythm: fresh beans and consumables restocked, a technician on-site for a quality visit, and an online analytics dashboard showing cups sold, revenue, stock and machine errors in real time. For high-volume, walk-up service we install Dr. Coffee and Necta super-automatics; for a barista-operated bar we install professional espresso equipment from La Spaziale, Nuova Simonelli, Rancilio, Iberital, Astoria or Fiorenzato. The coffee is fresh-roasted premium blends from Covim S.p.A. (Italy) and Ukrainian roasting partners — Bellissima, LOT 101, Ethiopia Yirgacheffe and more — matched and dosed to your machine, not a generic default.
