Barista staff training for office coffee setups

Direct answer

Staff and barista training is included with every Anima Volitiva rental at no extra charge. There is no fixed course length — the training is sized to your team and your machine, whether that is a Dr. Coffee or Necta super-automatic in an office or a professional espresso machine behind a HoReCa bar.

Training is a formal step in the five-stage onboarding: planning, setup, training, a first-week quality check, and a first-month review. The team learns to operate the machine, keep drink quality consistent, and handle daily care — and the first-week check verifies that what was taught is actually working in practice.

Knowledge does not go stale afterwards: a technician visits weekly to check quality and can coach staff on the spot, and the 24/7 support line answers operational questions between visits.
