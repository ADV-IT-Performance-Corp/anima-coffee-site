Premium super-automatic vs commercial espresso machines

Direct answer

The real question is who will make the coffee. A super-automatic grinds, doses and brews at the touch of a button, so it suits offices, retail and self-service points with no barista. A commercial espresso machine gives a trained barista full control over extraction and milk, so it belongs in cafes and restaurants. Neither is universally better — the format of the venue decides.

Anima Volitiva's lineup does not include the imported premium-brand machines often named in this comparison. For self-service and office settings it installs Dr. Coffee super-automatics (Minibar, Coffeebar, F12, Coffee Center, Coffee Zone, M12) and Necta models (Koro Prime, Korinto Prime, Krea Touch). For barista-operated HoReCa it installs professional espresso machines: La Spaziale S9, Nuova Simonelli Appia II, Rancilio Classe 7, Iberital IB7, Astoria Start and Fiorenzato F64.
