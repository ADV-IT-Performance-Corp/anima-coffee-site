Specialty coffee bean supply for offices and HoReCa

Direct answer

An honest note first: Anima Volitiva does not market its beans as certified specialty or single-origin micro-lots. What it supplies is premium roasted coffee from Covim S.p.A. (Italy) and Ukrainian roasting partners — blends such as Bellissima, SS (chocolate, nuts, citrus and jasmine notes, medium roast), LOT 101, LOT 105, Magnifica, Ethiopia Yirgacheffe, Colombia Excelso Decaf, Ambra and Ametista.

For offices and HoReCa the beans come as part of a managed supply, not a standalone bag order. One weekly delivery brings coffee, tea and the full set of consumables — cups, sugar, syrups, napkins — and a technician visits weekly to check drink quality on your machine, so the beans are actually dialled in rather than just dropped off.

Commercially it works on an individual quote for your volume, with a free 14-day trial without prepayment to taste the blends with your team or guests. HoReCa clients get the first rental month free. To choose blends and volumes, call +38 (073) 873 01 45 or write to animacoffeeco@gmail.com.
