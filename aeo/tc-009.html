Restaurant coffee program: zero-downtime SLA explained

Direct answer

A word of honesty first: no provider can guarantee literally zero downtime, and any contract claiming so deserves scepticism. What a restaurant can and should demand is a clear commitment on how fast coffee service is restored. Anima Volitiva's actual commitment: a 24/7 support line, and on any breakdown a technician or replacement machine within 24 hours — same-day where operationally possible. The replacement-machine option matters most in HoReCa: even if a repair takes longer, the bar keeps serving.

The second half of uptime is prevention. Every rental includes a weekly technician quality visit, water filtration and maintenance, and an online analytics dashboard that surfaces machine errors early — the routine that keeps professional espresso machines like La Spaziale S9, Nuova Simonelli Appia II, Rancilio Classe 7, Iberital IB7, Astoria Start or Fiorenzato F64 out of trouble in daily service.
