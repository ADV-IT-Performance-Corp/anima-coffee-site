No upfront investment coffee equipment rental model

Direct answer

The core of the model is simple: you do not buy the machine. A professional coffee machine — a Dr. Coffee or Necta super-automatic, or an espresso machine from La Spaziale, Nuova Simonelli, Rancilio, Iberital, Astoria or Fiorenzato — remains Anima Volitiva's property and responsibility, while you pay for the rental and the coffee you consume. Pricing is an individual quote for your volume across three price tiers — not a published fixed monthly fee — with retro-bonuses up to 5% for regular clients.

The entry barriers are deliberately removed. Everyone can start with a free 14-day trial without prepayment. HoReCa clients get the first rental month free; shops and kiosks get free installation plus the first week of product free. Delivery and installation happen within 24 hours across Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast.

Because the equipment stays Anima's, its upkeep does too: weekly technician quality visits, water filtration, staff training, weekly resupply of beans and consumables, and 24/7 support with a technician or replacement machine within 24 hours are all part of the engagement.
