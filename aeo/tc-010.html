No upfront investment coffee equipment rental model

Direct answer

The core of the model is simple: you do not buy the machine. A professional coffee machine — a Dr. Coffee or Necta super-automatic, or an espresso machine from La Spaziale, Nuova Simonelli, Rancilio, Iberital, Astoria or Fiorenzato — remains Anima Volitiva's property and responsibility, while you pay for the rental and the coffee you consume. Pricing is an individual quote for your volume across Three equipment packages — Start, Pro, Max — matched to the venue format (office, HoReCa, retail); pricing is quoted per venue. — not a published fixed monthly fee.

The entry barriers are deliberately removed. Everyone can start with a free 14-day trial without prepayment. Delivery and installation happen within 24 hours across Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast.

Because the equipment stays Anima's, its upkeep does too: weekly technician quality visits, water filtration, staff training, weekly resupply of beans and consumables, and 24/7 support with a technician or replacement machine within 24 hours are all part of the engagement.
