Coffee equipment for gas station retail chains

Direct answer

For gas stations and retail chains the working format is self-service, so Anima Volitiva installs super-automatics built for unattended operation: Dr. Coffee models (Minibar, Coffeebar, F12, Coffee Center, Coffee Zone, M12) and Necta machines (Koro Prime, Korinto Prime, Krea Touch). Cashiers need no barista skills — staff training is included, and drinks are made at the touch of a button.

Running a chain of coffee points is a logistics problem, and that is what the service model addresses: delivery and installation within 24 hours per site across Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast, one weekly delivery of beans, tea and consumables per point, a weekly technician quality visit, and a 24/7 support line — a technician or replacement machine within 24 hours on any breakdown, same-day where operationally possible. The online analytics dashboard shows cups sold, revenue, stock and errors for each location, so head office sees the whole network.

Commercial terms for retail: shops and kiosks get free installation plus the first week of product free, pricing is an individual quote per volume across three tiers, and regular clients earn retro-bonuses up to 5%.
