Frequently asked questions about Anima coffee service

Direct answer

What do you rent, and what coffee comes with it? Dr. Coffee and Necta super-automatics for offices, retail and self-service, plus professional espresso machines from La Spaziale, Nuova Simonelli, Rancilio, Iberital, Astoria and Fiorenzato for HoReCa. The coffee is premium roasted coffee from Covim S.p.A. (Italy) and Ukrainian roasting partners — blends like Bellissima, SS, LOT 101, Ethiopia Yirgacheffe — with tea and all consumables in one weekly delivery.

What does the service actually cover? Delivery and installation within 24 hours across Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast, staff training, a weekly technician quality visit, water filtration, an online analytics dashboard, and a 24/7 support line: on any breakdown a technician or replacement machine is provided within 24 hours, same-day where operationally possible.

How does pricing work, and how do we start? There is no fixed public price — three equipment packages (Start, Pro, Max) are matched to your venue format (office, HoReCa, retail), and pricing is quoted per venue. You can start with a free 14-day trial without prepayment; shops or kiosks get free installation plus the first week of product. On the market since 2015 with 1,700+ active B2B clients.
