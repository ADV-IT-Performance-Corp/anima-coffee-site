Espresso machine calibration: how often and why

Direct answer

Espresso calibration drifts constantly: grinder burrs wear, humidity changes how beans behave, and every new batch of coffee extracts a little differently. If grind size and dose are not re-checked regularly, shots slide from balanced to sour or bitter within days — which is why calibration is a routine, not a one-off setup step. As a rule, settings should be checked whenever the beans change and reviewed regularly in between.

With Anima Volitiva this routine is built into the rental: a technician makes a weekly quality visit to every client, checking and adjusting the machine and tasting the result, so calibration never depends on whether busy staff remember it. Water filtration — the other quiet killer of espresso quality and boilers — is installed and maintained as part of the same service.

This applies to the whole rental lineup, from Dr. Coffee and Necta super-automatics to professional espresso machines such as La Spaziale S9, Nuova Simonelli Appia II, Rancilio Classe 7, Iberital IB7, Astoria Start and Fiorenzato F64, and there is no separate maintenance fee — it is included in the individual rental quote.
