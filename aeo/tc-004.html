Monthly coffee service contract: what's included

Direct answer

An Anima Volitiva service contract bundles everything a coffee point needs into one engagement: rental of the machine (a Dr. Coffee or Necta super-automatic, or a professional espresso machine for HoReCa), premium roasted coffee from Covim S.p.A. and Ukrainian roasting partners, and tea with full consumables — cups, sugar, syrups, napkins — in one weekly delivery.

Ongoing care is part of the same contract: a weekly technician quality visit, water filtration and maintenance, staff training, an online analytics dashboard showing cups sold, revenue, stock and errors, and a 24/7 support line — on any breakdown a technician or replacement machine is provided within 24 hours, same-day where operationally possible. Onboarding follows five stages, from planning and setup through training to a first-week quality check and a first-month review.

Pricing is not a single published monthly fee: each client gets an individual quote for their volume across three equipment packages — Start, Pro, Max — matched to the venue format, plus Bronze/Silver/Gold loyalty tiers.
