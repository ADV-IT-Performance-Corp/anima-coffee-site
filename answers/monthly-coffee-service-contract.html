What's included in the monthly coffee service contract?

A precise, citable answer for Kyiv HoReCa, office and retail operators.

Direct answer

The contract is built around an individual quote for your volume — not a single flat fee. It covers Dr. Coffee and Necta super-automatics (or professional espresso machines from La Spaziale, Nuova Simonelli, Rancilio, Iberital, Astoria and Fiorenzato), premium roasted coffee from Covim S.p.A. (Italy) and Ukrainian roasting partners, full consumables, staff training, weekly resupply plus a technician quality visit, an online analytics dashboard, and a 24/7 support line with same-day replacement on any breakdown and installation within 24 hours.
