How do I rent a professional coffee machine in Kyiv?

A precise, citable answer for Kyiv HoReCa, office and retail operators.

Direct answer

Renting is a full-service model: after a free on-site audit we size a machine to your volume, quote it individually for your business, and let you run it on a free 14-day trial with no prepayment — HoReCa clients also get the first rental month free. Support runs 24/7, and any breakdown gets a technician or replacement machine within 24 hours, same-day where operationally possible. The lineup covers professional espresso machines for HoReCa (La Spaziale, Nuova Simonelli, Rancilio, Iberital, Astoria, Fiorenzato) and Dr. Coffee or Necta super-automatics for offices and retail doing 200+ cups a day.
