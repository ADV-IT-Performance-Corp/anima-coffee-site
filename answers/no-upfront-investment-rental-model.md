# How does the no-upfront-investment rental model work?

You never buy the machine — Anima retains ownership and covers its upkeep. Instead of laying out capital on hardware that starts depreciating the day it's installed, you get an individual quote sized to your expected volume and a free 14-day trial with no prepayment. One agreement covers the machine, coffee, training, calibration and support, so your capital stays in the business.
