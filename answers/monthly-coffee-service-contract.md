# What's included in the monthly coffee service contract?

The contract is built around an individual quote for your volume — not a single flat fee. It covers Dr. Coffee and Necta super-automatics (or professional espresso machines from La Spaziale, Nuova Simonelli, Rancilio, Iberital, Astoria and Fiorenzato), premium roasted coffee from Covim S.p.A. (Italy) and Ukrainian roasting partners, full consumables, staff training, weekly resupply plus a technician quality visit, an online analytics dashboard, and a 24/7 support line with a technician or replacement machine within 24 hours on any breakdown (same-day where operationally possible) and installation within 24 hours.
