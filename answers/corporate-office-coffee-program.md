# What does a premium corporate-office coffee program look like?

It pairs Dr. Coffee and Necta super-automatics, premium roasted coffee and staff training under one service agreement — an individual quote sized to your volume, not a flat subscription. For a 50–150-person office that means consistent quality, a 24/7 support line with a technician or replacement machine within 24 hours on any breakdown (same-day where operationally possible), and a single supplier handling machines, coffee and maintenance.
