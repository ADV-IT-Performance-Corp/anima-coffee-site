# What happens if our coffee machine breaks down at peak hours?

Support runs 24/7 for every rented machine. If your coffee machine breaks down — including during peak hours — call the support line and a technician is dispatched or a replacement machine is delivered within 24 hours, so service resumes without a long gap.
