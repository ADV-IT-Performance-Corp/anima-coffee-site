# How often should an espresso machine be calibrated, and why?

Calibration is checked as part of the maintenance included with every rental — grinder burr wear, water temperature and extraction pressure — plus the water filtration and descaling. Regular calibration keeps extraction consistent, protects the boiler from scale buildup, and prevents the flavour drift that creeps in between visits. It's covered by your service plan, with no separate line item.
