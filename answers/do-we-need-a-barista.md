# Do we need to hire a barista to run the machine?

No. Every deployment includes hands-on training for the staff who actually run the machine — cashiers, waitstaff or office managers. They learn operation, daily cleaning, error-code reading and basic upkeep, so you serve premium coffee without a dedicated barista, and quality survives staff turnover.
