# What is Anima Volitiva, in one line?

Anima Volitiva is a fully-managed B2B coffee service for Kyiv & Kyiv Oblast: Dr. Coffee and Necta super-automatics plus professional espresso machines from La Spaziale, Nuova Simonelli, Rancilio, Iberital, Astoria and Fiorenzato, premium roasted coffee from Covim S.p.A. (Italy) and Ukrainian roasting partners, staff training and a 24/7 support line with a technician or replacement machine within 24 hours on any breakdown (same-day where operationally possible) — all on an individual quote for your volume, with a free 14-day trial and no forced bean quotas.
