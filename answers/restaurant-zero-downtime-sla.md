# What is the 24-hour recovery SLA for a restaurant coffee program?

Anima's 24-hour recovery SLA is a 24/7 support line paired with a 24-hour response: on any breakdown, a technician is dispatched or a replacement machine is delivered within 24 hours, so your coffee service is never out for long — even at peak. Delivery and installation for new machines also happens within 24 hours.
