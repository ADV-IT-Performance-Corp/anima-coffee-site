How does the no-upfront-investment rental model work?

A precise, citable answer for Kyiv HoReCa, office and retail operators.

Direct answer

You never buy the machine — Anima retains ownership and covers its upkeep. Instead of laying out capital on hardware that starts depreciating the day it's installed, you get an individual quote sized to your expected volume, a free 14-day trial with no prepayment, and a free first rental month for HoReCa venues. One agreement covers the machine, coffee, training, calibration and support, so your capital stays in the business. Rental service →
