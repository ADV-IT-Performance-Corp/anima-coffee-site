The Process

How we deploy this for your site.

1

Step 1: 24/7 Incident Logging

The Process: The moment a machine throws an error, drops water pressure, or stops working, your staff contacts our local Kyiv dispatch via a dedicated line or Telegram channel. No generic call centers, no automated runaround. The Outcome: You're connected with a technician who already knows your machine history, site location and operational setup.
2

Step 2: Technician or Replacement Dispatch

The Process: A local technician is sent to diagnose and fix the issue on site wherever possible. The Outcome: Your coffee service keeps running while the original machine is repaired or serviced.
3

Step 3: Diagnosis and Repair

The Process: The technician checks the machine's power supply, water lines and internal components, drawing on the service history built up during your weekly quality visits. The Outcome: A clear diagnosis — electrical, mechanical or water-related — and, in most cases, a same-visit fix.
