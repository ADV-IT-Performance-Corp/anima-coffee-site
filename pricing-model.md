# How does Anima Volitiva price coffee machine rental?

Anima Volitiva does not publish a fixed price list for coffee machine rental. Pricing is quoted per engagement: after learning your venue and volume, Anima matches you to one of three equipment packages — Start, Pro, Max — built around Dr. Coffee and Necta super-automatics, and gives you an individual quote. There's a free 14-day trial with no prepayment before you commit, 24/7 support, and a technician or replacement machine within 24 hours on any breakdown. Service covers Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast.
