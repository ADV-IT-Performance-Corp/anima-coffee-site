Which coffee machine for an office, a kiosk or a restaurant: a venue-by-venue guide

Super-automatic or professional espresso machine? A practical guide for Kyiv offices, shops, kiosks and restaurants: which questions decide the choice and why the equipment package is matched to the venue.

The most common mistake when renting coffee equipment is choosing the machine first and the venue second. An office, a kiosk and a restaurant use coffee in completely different ways, so the same machine that is perfect for one can be a constant headache in another.

Below are the questions that actually decide the choice. We answer them from what Anima Volitiva installs, so you can compare like with like with any other supplier.

Who makes the coffee: a barista or the guest?

This is the first and most important question. If the drink is prepared by a trained barista, a professional espresso machine gives full control over the result. If the guest or the employee presses a button, a super-automatic machine is the practical answer, because it grinds, doses and brews the same way every time.

Office: a super-automatic with simple daily routine

In an office nobody wants to learn extraction. A super-automatic, such as the Dr. Coffee or Necta models we install, lets staff make a drink in a few touches, and the routine is limited to filling and cleaning. Staff training is included, so the people who use the machine every day know exactly what to do.

Kiosk, shop or self-service point: reliability and a short queue

Here the machine stands in front of customers all day and cannot wait for a technician. A super-automatic suits self-service and retail, and the service model matters as much as the model of the machine: a 24/7 support line and a technician on site or a replacement machine within 24 hours.

Restaurant, cafe or hotel: when a barista runs the bar

For HoReCa venues with a barista, we install professional espresso machines from La Spaziale, Nuova Simonelli, Rancilio, Iberital, Astoria and Fiorenzato. They give a trained barista room to work on the drink, but they need a trained person behind them, which is why we run staff and barista training as part of onboarding.

Do not forget water, coffee and consumables

Equipment is only a part of the cup. Water filtration and maintenance are included in our service, premium roasted coffee is resupplied weekly, and tea, cups, sugar, syrups and napkins can come in the same delivery, so one supplier is responsible for the whole result.

Why the equipment package follows the venue

We do not publish a flat price because a kiosk, an office and a restaurant need different equipment. Every client gets an individual quote and one of three equipment packages, Start, Pro or Max, matched to the venue format. Ask any supplier what exactly is included before you compare numbers.

How to decide in practice

Describe your venue in three lines: who makes the drink, how many people are served at the busiest hour, and where the machine will stand. With that, a supplier can propose equipment, and you can test it on your own volume with a free 14-day trial without prepayment.
