# What is the Staff Training Playbook for Commercial Coffee?

The Staff Training Playbook by Anima Volitiva is a structured, hands-on onboarding program designed to eliminate coffee waste, protect equipment, and halt "quality drift" in high-turnover B2B environments. It turns inexperienced retail and office staff into confident operators of our Dr. Coffee and Necta super-automatics and professional espresso machines.
