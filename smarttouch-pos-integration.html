Why Revenue Tracking Matters for High-Volume Coffee

When a coffee machine and your point of sale run as two separate, unconnected systems, cups poured and cups paid for can quietly drift apart — an expensive gap for any high-volume site, whether it's an office, a retail counter or a self-service corner. Anima Volitiva's machines feed cup counts, revenue and stock levels straight into an online analytics dashboard, so you can check that data against your POS reports and catch a discrepancy early, without adding anyone to watch the till. How the Integration Gets Set Up

Before a single machine is installed, our team walks through your site, your expected volume and your existing POS setup with you, so the machine and consumables lineup actually matches how you sell coffee.
