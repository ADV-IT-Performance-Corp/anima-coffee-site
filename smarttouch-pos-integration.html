    ### How does the Syrve (iiko) automated reconciliation work in real-time?

    Our Swiss-grade super-automatic coffee machines (Franke and WMF) are configured with industrial IoT telemetry cards. Every time an espresso, Americano, or flat white is brewed, the machine transmits the exact grind weight, extraction duration, and water temperature to our secure cloud middleware. Our middleware queries your Syrve (iiko) API to verify that a corresponding transaction has been registered. If a drink is brewed without an active, closed check, the mismatch is instantly flagged on your operational discrepancy log.

    ### Do we need to buy our own telemetry hardware or pay for integration?

    No. When you choose our premium managed coffee services under our comprehensive **Growth Bundle [$1,997/mo]**, all telemetry modules, Swiss-grade super-automatics, and API middleware licensing are included. There are no hidden setup fees, deposit requirements, or surprise line items.

    ### What happens if the internet goes down at our Kyiv venue?

    The IoT telemetry cards on our Swiss machines feature built-in industrial flash memory. In the event of a local network disruption, the card caches up to 10,000 physical brew events. Once your internet connection is restored, the system automatically synchronizes the backlog with your Syrve database, back-filling your reconciliation ledger with zero data loss.

    ### Can the integration detect if baristas are pouring double-shots but ringing up singles?

    Yes, absolutely. The telemetry system analyzes physical grind times and water volumes with 0.1-gram precision. If a double-shot of espresso is ground (utilizing 16 to 18 grams of coffee) but a single shot (utilizing 8 to 9 grams) is processed at your Syrve register, the system flags the exact volume mismatch, preventing baristas from pocketing transaction differences.

    ### How does Kyiv's hard water affect the reconciliation accuracy?

    Kyiv’s high municipal water hardness (15 to 20 dH) causes rapid calcium buildup inside standard water lines, which alters flow-meter readings and grinder calibration over time. To counter this, Anima installs multi-stage, ion-exchange water softeners with every machine. Our telemetry system monitors water pressure resistance, allowing us to send a technician to replace filtration media before scaling affects extraction metrics.

    ### Is this integration compatible with both Syrve Cloud and Syrve Server?

    Yes. Our custom middleware connects with both cloud-hosted (Syrve Cloud) and local on-premises installations (Syrve Server) via secure, lightweight API endpoints, maintaining stable data transfers without slowing down your local POS networks.

    ### How long does the initial onboarding and integration setup take?

    We can complete your physical site preparation, equipment installation, and API integration within 48 business hours of contract approval. We also conduct our mandatory 2-hour practical staff training session on-site to ensure your operations team is fully aligned on Day 1.
