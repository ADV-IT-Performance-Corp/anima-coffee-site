  • Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    **What exactly is included in the full-service rental package?**

    The package covers the rental of a Dr. Coffee or Necta super-automatic (or a professional espresso machine for barista-operated venues), a weekly supply of premium roasted coffee, tea and consumables, 24/7 technical support, a weekly technician quality visit, water filtration, staff training and an online analytics dashboard. Pricing is an individual quote for your volume.

    **Do we have to pay any upfront capital to install the machines?**

    No. You rent the equipment instead of buying it, and you can start with a free 14-day trial with no prepayment. Shops and kiosks get free installation plus the first week of product free.

    **How quickly will technical support arrive if our machine breaks down?**

    Our support line works 24/7. On any breakdown, a technician arrives within 24 hours or a replacement machine is provided — same-day service is available where operationally possible — for all clients within our Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast service coverage area.

    **What is covered in the barista staff training program?**

    Staff training is included with every installation. It covers the precise operation, daily cleaning protocols, and basic troubleshooting of the equipment, so your team can consistently serve quality drinks without damaging the machinery. Training is part of our 5-stage onboarding, which ends with a first-week quality check and a first-month review.

    **Is premium coffee included in the package?**

    Yes. You do not need to source your own coffee. We supply premium roasted coffee from Covim S.p.A. (Italy) and Ukrainian roasting partners — blends like Bellissima, LOT 101 and Ethiopia Yirgacheffe — replenished weekly together with tea and consumables.

    **How does a premium super-automatic machine compare to a traditional commercial espresso machine for HoReCa?**

    Super-automatics, like the Dr. Coffee and Necta models we provide, remove user error from the equation and deliver consistent drinks at high volumes without requiring an expert barista on staff. For barista-operated venues that want manual control, we install professional espresso machines such as the La Spaziale S9, Nuova Simonelli Appia II or Rancilio Classe 7 instead.

    **What happens during the weekly technician quality visit?**

    During the weekly visit, a trained technician inspects the machine, checks the grinder settings, water filtration, temperature and pressure, and verifies that drink quality matches the profile of the blend you are currently serving — alongside delivering your weekly resupply.

    **Is your 24/7 technical support available for all businesses in the Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast coverage area?**

    Yes. If your business is located within our defined service coverage area of Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast, you are fully covered by our 24/7 technical support network and the 24-hour technician-or-replacement guarantee.
