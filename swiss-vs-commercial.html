Answers

Frequently asked questions.

What's actually included when we work with Anima Volitiva?

An individual quote sized to your volume, the machine itself (super-automatic or professional espresso, matched to your venue), staff training, weekly resupply of beans and consumables, weekly quality visits, an online analytics dashboard, and a 24/7 support line with a technician or replacement machine within 24 hours if anything breaks.

Do you install Swiss brands like Franke or WMF?

No. Franke and WMF are premium European brands some vendors market as "Swiss-grade," but they're not what we install. Anima Volitiva runs Dr. Coffee and Necta super-automatics for offices, retail and self-service, plus professional espresso machines from La Spaziale, Nuova Simonelli, Rancilio, Iberital, Astoria and Fiorenzato for barista-run HoReCa venues — chosen for reliability and serviceability, not brand prestige.

How quickly will support respond if our machine breaks down?

Our support line runs 24/7. On a breakdown, we get a technician out or a replacement machine in place within 24 hours, same-day wherever possible. We don't promise a fixed clock-time window — we promise you won't be without coffee for long.

Is staff training included?

Yes, with every installation. We train your staff or baristas on daily operation, cleaning and basic troubleshooting, and stay until the team is comfortable — there's no fixed time limit on it.

What kind of coffee do you supply?

Premium roasted coffee from Covim S.p.A. (Italy) and Ukrainian roasting partners — named blends like Bellissima, LOT 101 and Ethiopia Yirgacheffe — roasted for consistent quality at commercial volume, plus tea and full consumables delivered in the same shipment.

How does a super-automatic compare to a professional espresso machine for HoReCa?

A super-automatic like our Dr. Coffee or Necta lineup grinds, doses and brews at the touch of a button — ideal for offices, retail and self-service points where no barista is on hand. A professional espresso machine like La Spaziale, Nuova Simonelli or Rancilio gives a trained barista full manual control over extraction — better suited to a HoReCa venue that wants hands-on quality. We help you match the machine to how your venue actually operates.

What happens during the weekly quality visit?

A technician restocks beans and consumables, checks grind and dose calibration, and tests the output to confirm the machine is still dialing in a consistent cup.

Is your 24/7 support line available everywhere you serve?

Yes — every client across Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast is covered by the same 24/7 support line and the same 24-hour technician-or-replacement commitment.
