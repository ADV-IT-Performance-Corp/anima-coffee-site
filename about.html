Who we are, what we promise, and exactly what's covered when you sign with us — in one place.

Direct answer

Anima Volitiva is a professional coffee equipment rental and full-service coffee company serving HoReCa, retail and corporate offices across Kyiv & Kyiv Oblast. We run one managed-infrastructure line — Dr. Coffee and Necta super-automatics plus professional espresso machines from La Spaziale, Nuova Simonelli, Rancilio, Iberital, Astoria and Fiorenzato, premium roasted coffee from Covim S.p.A. (Italy) and Ukrainian roasting partners, staff training and a 24/7 support line with a technician or replacement machine within 24 hours on any breakdown (same-day where operationally possible) — priced with an individual quote for your volume, a free 14-day trial with no prepayment, and no forced bean quotas.
