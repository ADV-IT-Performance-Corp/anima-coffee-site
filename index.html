Answers

Frequently asked questions.

What happens if the machine breaks down during a morning rush?

You call our 24/7 support line. If the issue can't be resolved remotely, we get a technician to you or deliver a replacement machine within 24 hours — often the same day — so your service doesn't stop for long.

Do we have to buy a minimum amount of coffee every month?

No. Your quote is based on your real volume across one of three pricing tiers, with retro-bonuses up to 5% for regular partners — there's no forced monthly minimum to hit.

What machines do you actually install?

Dr. Coffee and Necta super-automatics for offices, retail and self-service sites, plus professional espresso machines from La Spaziale, Nuova Simonelli, Rancilio, Iberital, Astoria and Fiorenzato for HoReCa venues with a barista on hand. We match the lineup to your traffic and space.

Is water filtration included, and will it protect the machine?

Yes. Water filtration and maintenance are included with every machine, and a technician visits weekly to check calibration and catch scale buildup before it becomes a repair.

Do we need to hire an experienced barista to operate the equipment?

No. Staff training is included with every installation, so cashiers, waitstaff or office managers can run the machine correctly from day one — no dedicated barista hire needed.

What's included in the service?

Machine rental, weekly resupply of beans and consumables, a weekly technician quality visit, water filtration and maintenance, staff training, a 24/7 support line, and an online dashboard showing cups sold, revenue, stock and errors — all priced by individual quote.

Why rent instead of buying a machine outright?

Buying ties up capital and puts every repair and part on you. Renting keeps the machine, coffee, service and support in one relationship — and you can try the full setup free for 14 days, with no prepayment, before committing.

How is pricing structured?

Every quote is individual, based on your volume and number of locations, across three price tiers — plus a free 14-day trial with no prepayment, a free first rental month for HoReCa clients, and free installation and first week of product for shops and kiosks.
