Protecting Coffee Machines from Kyiv Oblast's Hard Water

Water across Kyiv Oblast is naturally hard, and left untreated it causes limescale to build up inside a machine's boiler and lines, cutting efficiency and eventually causing breakdowns. Anima Volitiva installs water filtration on every machine we place and includes it in our managed service, together with weekly technician visits that check filtration and descale as needed. Our fleet includes Dr. Coffee and Necta super-automatics and professional espresso machines from La Spaziale, Nuova Simonelli, Rancilio, Iberital, Astoria and Fiorenzato — all built for continuous commercial use, but none of them are immune to limescale from untreated hard water.

  • 01
    Prevents Boiler Scale Buildup

    Filtration removes the calcium and magnesium that cause limescale, helping you avoid costly repairs and unplanned downtime.

  • 02
    Protects Coffee Quality

    Balanced water keeps our premium roasted coffee — from Covim S.p.A. (Italy) and Ukrainian roasting partners — tasting the way it should, without the flat or bitter notes hard water can cause.

  • 03
    Reduces Wear on Heating Elements

    Clean components run efficiently and last longer, instead of working harder against a growing layer of scale.

  • 04
    Included in Every Rental

    Water filtration, weekly technician visits and resupply of beans and consumables are all part of the service — no separate invoice to manage.

  • 05
    Backed by 24/7 Support

    If anything with your machine's water line or filtration needs attention, our support line is open around the clock, with a technician or replacement machine on-site within 24 hours.
