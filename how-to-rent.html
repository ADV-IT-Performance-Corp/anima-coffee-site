Answers

Rental questions.

What happens if the rented coffee machine breaks down during a busy shift?

Call our 24/7 support line. We send a technician or a replacement machine within 24 hours — same-day where possible — so you're never stuck without a working machine for long.

Are maintenance and water-filter replacements included?

Yes. Every rental includes water filtration and maintenance, plus a weekly technician quality visit — not just a reaction when something breaks.

If my business outgrows the machine, can we upgrade, and how does that affect the contract?

Yes. We size deployments consultatively, and if your traffic grows we can move you to a higher-capacity super-automatic or a multi-group professional espresso machine — your account manager handles the transition.

Do we have to buy coffee exclusively from you?

Coffee, consumables and equipment come as one bundle with weekly resupply — that's the model. Pricing is individually quoted per client across three tiers, with retro-bonuses up to 5% for regular partners.

Is installation and staff training provided on delivery?

Yes. Delivery and installation happen within 24 hours of signing, and our team trains your staff on dosing, grinding and day-to-day machine care — no rigid time limit, until they're confident.

How much does rental cost?

There's no flat published price — we quote individually based on your volume and site, across three pricing tiers, with a free 14-day trial before you commit and a free first rental month for HoReCa clients.

How quickly can a technician reach us in Kyiv or Kyiv Oblast on total equipment failure?

Our support line is available 24/7, and we guarantee a technician or replacement machine within 24 hours — same-day where possible — so you're never left facing extended downtime.
