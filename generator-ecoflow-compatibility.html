  • 8. FAQ SECTION

    ### What are the exact electrical outlet specifications for a WMF super-automatic?

    Most commercial WMF super-automatic machines require a dedicated, single-phase 220V–240V Schuko socket rated for 16 Amps (continuous draw of 2.75 kW to 3.5 kW). This outlet must run on an isolated circuit breaker to prevent electrical overloads when other high-power breakroom appliances are in use.

    ### Do I need a direct water connection, or can the machine run from an internal water tank?

    While some WMF models feature integrated water tanks, high-demand office, HoReCa, and retail environments require a direct water line connection. This direct setup utilizes a 3/8" Male Thread connection with an accessible shut-off valve within 1 meter of the machine, operating at a regulated dynamic pressure of 1.5 to 6 bar.

    ### How hard is Kyiv's municipal water, and how does it affect my machine?

    Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast municipal water is very hard, routinely measuring between 15 and 20 dH. This high mineral content leads to rapid scale formation in boiler systems. To keep your system running and protect your setup, we include and calibrate a professional decarbonizing bypass filter with every installation, maintaining an optimal hardness of 4–6 dH.

    ### Can the wastewater drain line run horizontally, or does it require a gradient?

    The wastewater line cannot run completely flat or horizontal. It must maintain a continuous downward slope of at least 2% (a 2 cm drop per meter of run) leading to a siphon-equipped drain. Any upward bends or flat runs will collect coffee grounds and milk fats, leading to line blockages and counter flooding.

    ### How much physical clearance space is required around a WMF machine?

    Your counter must support a dry machine weight of up to 40 kg and offer a minimum physical width of 380mm to 450mm. You must also allow at least 50mm of clear space on both sides for ventilation and 200mm to 300mm of open space above the hoppers to make daily refilling simple and safe.

    ### What happens if our facility experiences a sudden voltage spike or power surge?

    Kyiv’s grid can experience voltage fluctuations that pose a risk to WMF's electronic controller boards. To protect against this, our team analyzes your electrical panel and installs commercial-grade voltage stabilizers and surge protection with every managed system.
