FAQ

Barista & staff training — answered.

Is training mandatory or optional?

It's included, not optional — every staff member who will operate the machine goes through it as part of deployment. There's no separate line item for it; it's part of the standard service.

What exactly does the session cover?

Precision operation, daily cleaning and descaling protocols, POS billing procedures, error-code recognition, and the safe power-transition sequence for blackouts.

Does training reduce bean waste?

Yes. Standardized grinder and temperature settings, plus a documented cleaning routine, consistently cut down the waste that comes from trial-and-error brewing and mis-dosed shots.

Does training protect the machine itself?

Yes — staff are trained on the correct shutdown and restart sequence for power transitions, which lowers the risk of hardware damage from panicked resets. And if something does go wrong, we repair or replace the machine within 24 hours.

Do you retrain when staff turn over?

Yes. As part of the ongoing relationship we retrain new hires so quality never depends on any one employee's tenure.
