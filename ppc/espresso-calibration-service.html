The weekly quality-visit protocol

What actually happens on a quality visit.

A defined checklist, not an ad-hoc once-over.

Grinder burr inspection & adjustment

FAQ

Espresso machine calibration — answered.

How often is calibration actually needed?

Weekly — during scheduled quality visits, our technician checks grinder burrs, water temperature and extraction pressure, plus descaling against Kyiv's hard water.

Is calibration a separate cost?

No — it's included with every Anima Volitiva rental or managed service, whether HoReCa, office or retail. There's no separate calibration invoice.

What does a calibration visit actually check?

Grinder burr wear, brew temperature, pump pressure, descaling and filtration status, and any developing error-code history.

What happens if the machine still faults between visits?

Our 24/7 support line is always open, and we provide a repair or replacement machine within 24 hours, with same-day service where possible.

Does calibration reduce bean waste?

Yes — properly calibrated grind size and extraction pressure reduce wasted beans and inconsistent shots compared with a machine that's never adjusted.
