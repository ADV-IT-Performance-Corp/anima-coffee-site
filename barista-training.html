What exactly is covered during barista staff training?

Our hands-on staff training, included with every deployment, covers the complete operation of your machine — whether that is a Dr. Coffee or Necta super-automatic or a professional espresso machine from La Spaziale, Nuova Simonelli, Rancilio, Iberital, Astoria or Fiorenzato. We teach your office or HoReCa staff proper extraction, daily cleaning, milk system maintenance and basic troubleshooting. This helps your coffee taste its best and prevents user-error breakdowns.

No. You do not need to purchase the machinery. We offer a free 14-day trial with no prepayment, and pricing is an individual quote sized to your volume — for HoReCa clients, the first rental month is free.

You are covered by our 24/7 support line. If a critical failure occurs, we get a technician out or a replacement machine to you within 24 hours, same-day wherever possible, anywhere within Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast, so you are not stuck without coffee for long.
