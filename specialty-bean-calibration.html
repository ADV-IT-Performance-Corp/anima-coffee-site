The Process

How we deploy this for your site.

1

Step 1: Site & Water Assessment

Before we install anything, we look at your location — water hardness, footfall, and where the machine will sit. This shapes the filtration and maintenance plan built for your site. *Outcome:* A calibration and maintenance plan built around your specific site, not a generic default.
2

Step 2: Grinder & Dose Calibration

We match the machine to your coffee — premium roasted blends from Covim S.p.A. (Italy) and Ukrainian roasting partners, such as Bellissima, LOT 101 or Ethiopia Yirgacheffe — and calibrate grinder burrs, dose and extraction time to that specific roast profile. *Outcome:* Consistent extraction from the first cup, without the burnt or sour shots that come from mismatched settings.
3

Step 3: Staff Training

Coffee quality slips when staff don't understand the machine. We train your team on daily cleaning, correct use, and why calibration settings shouldn't be touched — no fixed clock on the session, we cover what your team needs. *Outcome:* Fewer user errors and no accidental drift in calibration.
4

Step 4: Weekly Quality Visits

Our technicians visit weekly to check calibration, top up beans and consumables, and inspect the filtration system — catching small issues before they turn into downtime. *Outcome:* Steady quality week after week, with problems caught early.
5

Step 5: 24/7 Support Protocol

If something drifts or breaks between visits, you call our 24/7 line. We dispatch a technician or a replacement machine within 24 hours, same-day where possible. *Outcome:* Minimal disruption — and a team that's always reachable. ***

Why Calibration and Maintenance Matter

Untreated hard water and a mismatched grind are the two most common causes of bad coffee and premature equipment wear. Left unfiltered, hard water scales the internals over time; left uncalibrated, a mismatched grind produces bitter or sour shots no matter how good the beans are. That's why water filtration and maintenance are included in every Anima Volitiva placement, and why our technicians visit weekly instead of waiting for a call. If something still goes wrong, our 24/7 line puts a technician or a replacement machine on-site within 24 hours. It's the same standard we've run since 2015, for the 1,700+ active B2B clients we currently serve — about 99% of whom stay with us.
