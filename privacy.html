Privacy & data notice

How we handle the information you send us through this site.

Who this is

This site is operated by Anima Volitiva, a B2B coffee equipment rental and service business based in Kyiv Oblast, Ukraine. Contact: animacoffeeco@gmail.com · +38 (073) 873 01 45.

What we collect

When you submit the assessment form on this site, we collect the fields you fill in — your name, company or venue, city, number of machines needed, an email or phone number to reach you, and any additional details you add. We also record which page you submitted from and, if you arrived via a tracked link, the campaign parameters (UTM values or a Google Ads click ID) that tell us how you found the site. We do not ask for or store any payment details on this site.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry, prepare a quote, and follow up about the service you asked about. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties. We may use a customer-relationship-management tool to organize incoming requests and, since you are contacting us to be reached about your inquiry, to follow up with you.

Analytics

We use Yandex Metrica to understand how visitors use this site — pages viewed and general on-site behavior. It does not receive the contents of the form you submit. We do not currently run Google Analytics on this site; if that changes, this page will be updated first.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry records for as long as needed to respond to you and, if you become a client, to service your account. You can ask us to delete your information at any time — see below.

Your rights

To review, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, email animacoffeeco@gmail.com with your request and we will respond directly.
